What does this graph show?
  • The graph visualizes pointer movement reported by the operating system/browser.
  • ΔX and ΔY are changes between consecutive recorded cursor positions.
  • Vectors show the direction and magnitude of those coordinate changes.
  • An optical mouse's internal sensor captures successive surface images and estimates relative movement.
  • This app does not read the mouse's internal sensor frames or raw hardware displacement.
